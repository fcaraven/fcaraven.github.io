Spatial zoom

The animations zoom toward the center of four samples of the SHF and their corresponding KPZ fields, then return to the initial view, a tilted square of macroscopic size 10×10. For each field, the four samples share the same initial vertical scale (approximately 0 to 184 for SHF and −5 to +5 for KPZ). As the zoom changes, the vertical scale adapts separately for each sample.
